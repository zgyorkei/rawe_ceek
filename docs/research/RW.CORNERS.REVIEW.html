rawe_ceek: the 30 iconic corners, for your review

No corner picture will be made until you approve this list. Each circuit has one proposed corner. For each row choose Keep, Change or Drop, and write a remark or another corner in the box if you like (the box is optional). At the end, copy the answers or save them as a text file and give them back.

Source: the research note RW.RESEARCH, section 4.6 (docs/research/RW.RESEARCH.md on the branch item/RW.RESEARCH, commit 2ee885e), facts fetched 2026-11-26. The fetch tool returned a summary of each page, so a quote was not checked against the raw page. The page works offline; your answers are also kept in this browser while you work.

Series: F1 and WEC are from the research's circuit table (4.4). "Commons" is Wikimedia Commons: only Monaco, Spa, Silverstone, Monza and Le Mans were searched; for every other circuit it was not checked.
